# Screenshots for the report

To take a screenshot on a Mac, press **Cmd + Shift + 4**, then **Space**, then click a window. That grabs the whole window cleanly.

Save each file into `report/screenshots/` with exactly the name below. Use `.png` or `.jpg`. Then run `python scripts/build_report.py`, and the screenshots are embedded automatically.

For phone screens, open the play page on your phone (or narrow the browser window) and take the screenshot there.

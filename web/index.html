        How it works

        • Professor Lux (AI host) runs the show. Archie, Coach Bea and Dr. Nova are AI contestants with their own strengths.
        • Code is the referee. Who buzzed first, scores, wagers and turns are all plain code. AIs only talk and propose.
        • The judge can't be sweet-talked. Answers are matched in code. An AI judge is only asked about close calls, and answers that try to give it orders are marked wrong.
        • Every Yale clue is fact-checked against a source you can open after the clue. Custom-topic boards are AI-written and labeled as not fact-checked.
        • Budget-safe. A daily spending cap and rate limits protect the server. When the cap is hit, the AIs keep playing in offline demo mode.

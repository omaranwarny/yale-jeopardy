How it keeps clues honest

1. Your material only. Hidden text in files (white or tiny text, hidden slides) is removed, and lines that try to instruct the AI are dropped before any model sees the material.

2. Quote or it doesn't ship. The writer agent must quote the exact line behind every clue. Code checks that the quote really is in your file, and a second agent fact-checks each clue.

3. You have the final say. Edit, swap or delete any clue before class, and overrule any answer during the game.
